neFaRec - Workout Tracker
Project Overview

Most people already have a routine, written in a note, a WhatsApp message or an AI chat. neFaRec starts there. You paste the text, check what it read on a review screen, and train it with a set table that remembers last time’s numbers. Everything is stored on the phone and works offline. An optional account backs it up to a small Node server.

Key Features
  • Paste a routine as plain text; the parser reads sets, rep ranges, timed holds and rest, and highlights anything it filled in.
  • Track sets against last session’s numbers, with a rest scoreboard that counts into overtime and a hold timer for planks and wall sits.
  • Follow the rest timer on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island with a Live Activity.
  • Review sessions as receipts, with weekly volume, muscle split and lift trend charts and a training calendar.
  • Browse 884 exercises with steps, photos and 42 looping demo videos, swap moves for your equipment, and try an on-device squat form check.
Open source

I published the routine parser as workout_parser on pub.dev, a pure Dart package any Flutter or Dart app can use. It reads days, sets, reps, holds, rest, weight, tempo and supersets, matches names against 884 exercises, and marks every value it filled in. It reads all 39 test routines with every checked field right, and a typical routine parses in about 10 ms.
