OtakuPlanner

A planner for anime fans. Tasks live on a calendar, recurring tasks are typed in plain English, and a daily streak, badges and a planning assistant keep you going. Anime mode is the second half: pick a character, and the whole planner takes their colours, their lines and their voice.

Project Overview

The phone on the left is the real app, screen by screen. Switch between Normal and Anime mode above it, or tap the screen to explore. In Anime mode the planner is printed in Luffy's colours, the line of the day is one of his, a finished streak gets a word from him, and the assistant plans your week in his voice.

Key Features
  • Custom calendar to add, edit and complete tasks, including recurring ones
  • Recurring tasks set up by typing them in plain English
  • Searchable tasks with custom categories saved per user
  • Reminders five minutes before a task and when it is due
  • Badges with levels and progress, and a daily streak
  • A planning assistant that suggests tasks you add with one tap
Anime mode
  • Pick any character on AniList, or start from the ones fans favourite most
  • The planner's ink and stamp are taken from their art, in light and dark
  • The line of the day comes from the character's own lines
  • Reminders and celebrations (a streak, a cleared day, a new badge) come with one of their lines
  • The assistant answers as them, with the same tasks, dates and times
  • Their portrait stands in for the mascot on empty days and in the app bar
How Anime mode works

Characters and art come from AniList's public API, which is free and needs no key. The colours are read on the phone with Google's Material colour utilities, then darkened or lightened until text is as readable as in the normal theme. Lines come from Animechan's free API, fetched once per character and kept on the phone, without stage directions or grim lines. For the voice, the app sends the character's name with each question and the Express backend adds it to the Gemini prompt; only a cleaned name gets through.
